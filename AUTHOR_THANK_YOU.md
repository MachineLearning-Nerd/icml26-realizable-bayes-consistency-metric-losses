# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Dan Tsir Cohen, Steve Hanneke, and Aryeh Kontorovich for making
this work available and for developing a sharp characterization of
realizable learning under general metric losses.

The paper connects finite combinatorial obstructions, distribution-free
learning, and the distinction between bounded and unbounded metric losses.
This repository is an independent, respectful audit: it records the finite
construction that was actually checked and keeps the infinite theorem and
remaining claims explicitly outside the evidence boundary.
